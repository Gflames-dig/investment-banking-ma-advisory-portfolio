## PROJECT 1: DISCOUNTED CASH FLOW VALUATION

### Objective

Develop a structured approach to estimating a company's value using projected future cash flows and an appropriate discount rate.

### Methodology

1. Establish the forecast period and document the underlying assumptions.
2. Project free cash flows using appropriate operating and financial assumptions.
3. Select a discount rate consistent with the cash flows being valued.
4. Calculate the present value of forecast cash flows.
5. Estimate terminal value using a clearly stated methodology.
6. Calculate enterprise value and account for relevant adjustments when deriving equity value.
7. Perform sensitivity analysis to examine how changes in assumptions affect the valuation.

### Quality-Control Checks

- Verify cash-flow calculations and discounting periods.
- Ensure consistency between cash flows and the discount rate.
- Review terminal growth and other material assumptions.
- Distinguish enterprise value from equity value.
- Check formulas and spreadsheet references for errors.
- Explain how changes in key inputs affect the final valuation.

### Expected Deliverable

A transparent valuation model containing documented assumptions, supporting calculations, and sensitivity analysis.

## PROJECT 2: COMPARABLE COMPANY ANALYSIS

### Objective

Evaluate a company's relative valuation using relevant publicly available information from comparable businesses.

### Methodology

1. Identify companies with similar business models, operating characteristics, and market exposure.
2. Collect relevant financial and operating information.
3. Normalize financial data where necessary to improve comparability.
4. Calculate appropriate valuation multiples, including EV/EBITDA and EV/Revenue where applicable.
5. Examine differences in profitability, growth, leverage, and business risk.
6. Apply justified valuation benchmarks to the company being evaluated.
7. Explain the limitations and uncertainties affecting the comparison.

### Quality-Control Checks

- Use consistent reporting periods and currency units.
- Distinguish enterprise-value multiples from equity-value multiples.
- Check the treatment of debt, cash, and other relevant adjustments.
- Investigate unusual valuation multiples.
- Document the rationale for selecting comparable companies.
- Avoid presenting estimates as verified market data.

### Expected Deliverable

A comparable-company analysis documenting the selection criteria, relevant financial metrics, valuation multiples, and resulting interpretation.

## PROJECT 3: MERGERS AND ACQUISITIONS TRANSACTION ANALYSIS

### Objective

Evaluate the financial and strategic considerations involved in a potential acquisition or business sale.

### Methodology

1. Establish the transaction objective and strategic rationale.
2. Review the target company's financial performance.
3. Identify potential revenue opportunities, cost efficiencies, and operational considerations.
4. Examine valuation assumptions and relevant transaction benchmarks.
5. Evaluate potential synergies and the assumptions required to achieve them.
6. Assess financial, operational, and execution risks.
7. Test alternative transaction scenarios.
8. Summarize the findings in a structured analytical report.

### Quality-Control Checks

- Distinguish verified information from assumptions.
- Check the consistency of valuation inputs and outputs.
- Avoid double-counting expected synergies.
- Identify information gaps requiring further investigation.
- Explain how changes in assumptions affect the transaction rationale.
- Ensure that recommendations follow logically from the available evidence.

### Expected Deliverable

A transaction-analysis report outlining the strategic rationale, valuation considerations, principal risks, and outstanding due-diligence questions.

## PROJECT 4: FINANCIAL DUE DILIGENCE AND MODEL REVIEW

### Objective

Establish a systematic process for identifying financial inconsistencies, unsupported assumptions, and weaknesses in analytical models.

### Review Framework

**1. Data Integrity**

Review source data, reporting periods, currency units, and the completeness of the financial information.

**2. Formula Accuracy**

Inspect spreadsheet formulas, cell references, linked assumptions, and calculation dependencies.

**3. Accounting Consistency**

Review the treatment of revenue, expenses, debt, cash, working capital, and other material financial items.

**4. Assumption Testing**

Assess whether revenue growth, margins, capital expenditure, discount rates, and other inputs are supported by reasonable explanations.

**5. Scenario Analysis**

Test how changes in key assumptions affect financial projections, valuation, and transaction conclusions.

**6. Findings and Recommendations**

Document each identified issue, explain its potential impact, and recommend an appropriate corrective action.

### Expected Deliverable

A structured review report distinguishing calculation errors, data-quality issues, unsupported assumptions, and weaknesses in the analytical approach.

## PROJECT 5: FINANCIAL REASONING AND AI MODEL EVALUATION

### Objective

Evaluate whether AI-generated financial analyses are numerically accurate, logically consistent, and appropriately supported by their assumptions.

### Evaluation Criteria

- Numerical and calculation accuracy
- Correct application of financial concepts
- Consistency between assumptions and conclusions
- Appropriate valuation methodology
- Recognition of uncertainty and limitations
- Clear explanations of calculations
- Relevance of recommendations to the available evidence

### Evaluation Process

1. Define the evaluation criteria and expected analytical outcome.
2. Review the AI-generated response and identify its principal claims.
3. Independently verify important calculations and assumptions.
4. Identify numerical errors, unsupported conclusions, and methodological weaknesses.
5. Document each issue with supporting evidence.
6. Explain the financial significance of each finding.
7. Provide actionable feedback and a corrected analytical approach.

### Expected Deliverable

An evaluation report documenting identified issues, supporting evidence, financial implications, and recommended corrections.
